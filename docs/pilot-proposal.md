# Customer pilot hypothesis — not validated customer discovery

**Prospective user:** a maintenance service provider inspecting multiple ventilation fans or blowers.

**Question:** could camera spot checks reduce setup effort for assets whose motion lies inside a verified measurement envelope?

**Trial:** agree on eligible assets and reference measurements, document capture conditions, collect repeated recordings, and measure setup plus analysis time. The present prototype must pass controlled physical validation before an industrial trial.

**Success criteria to negotiate:** frequency agreement tolerance, repeatability, usable-recording fraction, total inspection time and usefulness of the report. No numerical industrial performance or savings are claimed yet.

**Economics:** inspections × minutes saved / 60 × hourly labor cost, minus training, recaptures, analysis and reference checks. Pilot pricing should follow measured delivery cost and customer willingness to pay; avoid unsupported downtime savings.

**Discovery questions:** How is vibration inspected today? Which setup step takes longest? What decisions follow a measurement? What evidence is required to adopt a new method? Which assets cannot be monitored adequately by camera?
